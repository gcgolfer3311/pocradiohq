Who runs POC Radio HQ — and why you should trust the reviews

Independent reviews from someone who actually uses radios — not a content farm, not a manufacturer front, not a list of specs scraped from Amazon. Here's the background behind the site and how we test.

Who we are

POC Radio HQ is operated by Michael Smith, a licensed amateur radio operator (callsign KM7FMJ) based in Gilbert, Arizona. Michael runs a network of independent radio review and resource sites covering the full spectrum of the radio hobby — shortwave listening, CB, GMRS, and now Push-to-Talk over Cellular.

The POC radio category caught our attention for a specific reason: it's a real communications tool that overlaps directly with the radio hobby community — shortwave listeners adding nationwide PTT capability, ham operators using Zello nets, preppers building dual-mode kits — but there was no independent, hobbyist-credible review site covering it. Everything we found was either a manufacturer reseller site or thin affiliate content with no real testing behind it. POC Radio HQ exists to fill that gap.

How we review

1

Hands-on testing where possible

We purchase or receive review units and test them in real-world conditions — PTT audio quality, range, battery life, app setup, SW/AM/FM reception where applicable. The ChoYong WT2 is our first fully hands-on POC review. More are in progress as we build out the review library.

2

Spec + owner-report research for other picks

For radios we haven't tested hands-on yet, we cross-reference manufacturer specs against verified owner reviews on Amazon, Reddit, and radio community forums. We flag which reviews are research-based vs. hands-on tested so you always know what you're reading.

3

One winner per category

We don't publish "Top 10" lists padded with filler. Each buying guide category has one pick — the specific radio we'd actually recommend for that use case — with a clear reason why and an honest accounting of its weaknesses.

4

Updated as gear changes

POC radios and their SIM/subscription terms change. We update picks and pricing notes when the market shifts rather than leaving stale recommendations live.

Affiliate & review unit disclosure
