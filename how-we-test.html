Method

How we test PoC radios

Short version: a licensed radio operator uses each radio on a live cell network, runs the same six checks every time, and publishes the cons alongside the pros. A radio we haven't used gets a spec-based overview and no score.

Who tests

Every bench-tested radio on this site is used by Michael Smith, a licensed amateur radio operator (KM7FMJ) in Arizona. Michael also runs HQ Shortwave Radio, part of the same HQ Radio Network, so radios here are judged by someone who uses conventional radios too and knows what PoC does and doesn't replace.

The bench checklist

Every radio that gets a score goes through the same six checks.

  1. Out of the box

    What's in the box, whether the SIM is included, and how long it takes to get from box to a first working call.

  2. Audio

    How clear and how loud voices are on real calls, at both ends.

  3. PTT delay

    The time from pressing the talk button to the other radio hearing you, timed with a stopwatch or on video. We started timing on the Radtel P8 (1 to 2 seconds) and are adding it to earlier radios as we re-test them. Until then the bench shows "not timed yet" rather than a guess.

  4. Build and screen

    How it feels in the hand, button feel, weight, and whether the screen is readable outdoors.

  5. App and groups

    How groups and contacts are set up, whether you can find other users, and anything buggy along the way.

  6. Real cost

    Hardware price plus the SIM or platform fee in year two and beyond, using the brand's published pricing. If a brand doesn't publish it, we say so instead of estimating.

How scores work

Scores are out of 5 and shown as signal bars. They're an overall judgement after hands-on use, weighing the checklist against the price.

4.5+Easy recommendation for its job
4.0Good, with trade-offs you should know
3.5Fine for the right buyer only
3.0Significant problems
<3We'd steer you elsewhere

We don't publish star ratings borrowed from store listings or "owner report" counts. If a radio hasn't been on the bench, it has no score.

Review units and money

Many radios are sent to us free by the manufacturer for review. Manufacturers don't get a vote on the verdict, and every review lists the cons. Some links on the site are affiliate links (for example Amazon, tag pocradio-20), which pay a commission at no extra cost to you. See the affiliate disclosure.

Corrections

If something on a review is wrong or out of date (prices, fees and bands change), tell us and we'll fix it.
