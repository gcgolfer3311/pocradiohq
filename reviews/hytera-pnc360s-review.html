Who it's best for

Small businesses managing delivery drivers, security patrols, or field service teams who need a manager-facing dispatch view, not just radio-to-radio calling.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Horizon dispatch console gives a manager a live map view of the entire team, not just point-to-point calling
  • Emergency SOS button sends an instant alert with GPS coordinates to dispatch
  • Cross-enterprise group calling lets you loop in radios from a partner company's Horizon account
  • Backed by an established LMR manufacturer with real support infrastructure

Cons

  • Monthly per-seat dispatch fee on top of hardware cost — not a one-time purchase
  • Overkill and overpriced for a single user or small family group
  • Setup requires more configuration than a consumer plug-and-play radio

Verdict

This is the pick once your use case becomes 'I need to know where my team is and broadcast to all of them at once' rather than 'I need a walkie-talkie.' The dispatch platform is the product; the radio is just the terminal.
