Who it's best for

Radio hobbyists and shortwave listeners who also want PoC capability, or anyone who'd rather carry one multi-function device than separate PoC, walkie-talkie, and receiver hardware.

Pros & Cons

Pros

  • Only device here that's also a legitimately good SW/MW/FM receiver — the TEF6686 chip has a strong reputation among radio hobbyists
  • PoC and analog UHF modes both work independently, so it's two communication tools plus a receiver in one
  • Internet radio access to 50,000+ stations turns dead time into a bonus feature, not filler
  • ChatGPT voice search for stations is a genuinely useful shortcut, not a gimmick

Cons

  • Analog side is only 2W / ~5km — a local backup, not a long-range RF radio like the GLOBAL-PTT 5288 Plus
  • Jack-of-all-trades design means none of its four functions is the single best in class at that function alone
  • Smaller, newer brand than Motorola/Hytera for buyers who prioritize enterprise support

Verdict

Not the pick if you need one thing done best — it's the pick if you want the most capability packed into a single handheld, and you're the kind of person who'd actually use the SW/internet radio side, not just the PTT function.
