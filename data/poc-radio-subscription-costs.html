PoC radio subscription costs: which have no monthly fee?

Updated November 2026 · Prices from each brand's published terms

The short answer
  • Most consumer PoC radios charge nothing monthly: year one is included, then a yearly SIM renewal of about $10 per radio.
  • No renewal at all: the CARRBORG X13 (lifetime free data, tested) and the GLOBAL-PTT 5288 Plus (listed with no subscription, not yet tested).
  • Cheapest to own: the Radtel P8, about $280 for 4 radios over three years.
  • Monthly per-user fees are mostly an enterprise thing: dispatch platforms like Hytera Horizon and Motorola WAVE PTX.

The takeaway: a renewal of about $10 a year barely moves the total. Over three years the purchase price matters far more than the subscription, so "lifetime free data" only wins on cost if the radio itself is priced close to the alternatives.

What "no monthly fee" really means

Yearly, not monthly. Most "no monthly fee" radios still renew once a year. At about $10 per radio that's fine, but it is a subscription.

"Lifetime" means the seller's lifetime. Free-for-life data and platforms depend on the seller and its PoC platform staying in business. Treat it as a strong discount, not a guarantee.

Data caps exist. TIDRADIO publishes 800 MB per month for the TD-M2 Pro. Many brands don't publish a cap at all. Voice PTT uses little data, but GPS tracking and video features add more.

Platform fees can hide elsewhere. Some vendors charge for extras like adding group members or renaming devices. Ask before you buy.

Questions to ask the seller before you buy

  1. What does renewal cost after year one, per SIM, and is it locked in?
  2. Is there a monthly data cap, and what happens when it runs out?
  3. Is the SIM locked to your platform, or can I use my own carrier?
  4. Are there fees for adding radios, users or groups later?
  5. If your company or platform shuts down, what happens to the radios?

Questions

Do PoC radios have a monthly fee?

Most consumer PoC radios don't charge monthly. The common model is a SIM included free for the first year, then a yearly renewal: TIDRADIO publishes $9.99 per year for the TD-M2 Pro and Radtel quotes about $10 per SIM per year for the P8. Some sellers, such as CARRBORG for the X13, advertise lifetime free data. Enterprise dispatch platforms like Hytera Horizon or Motorola WAVE PTX usually bill per user per month.

Which PoC radio has no subscription at all?

Of the radios we've tested, the CARRBORG X13 is sold with lifetime free data, so there is no renewal to pay. The GLOBAL-PTT 5288 Plus is also listed with no recurring subscription, but we haven't tested it yet. Lifetime claims last only as long as the seller and its platform stay in business.

What is the cheapest PoC radio to own over three years?

For a 4-radio team using published pricing, the Radtel P8 is cheapest at about $280 over three years (about $50 per radio plus about $10 per SIM per year after year one), followed by the TIDRADIO TD-M2 Pro at about $340 and the CARRBORG X13 at about $620 with no renewals.

Can I use my own SIM card in a PoC radio?

Often, yes. Radtel sells the P8 with or without an included SIM; without it you supply your own AT&T, T-Mobile or Verizon SIM and pay that carrier's data rate. Check the radio's LTE bands against your carrier before buying.
